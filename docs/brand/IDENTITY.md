# Life identity

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. The finished mark is a folded leaf ribbon
suggesting a lowercase l, on an evergreen background. The app pairs mint highlights with a
coral pulse line. The generated raster was resized to 1024 × 1024 for the iOS asset catalog.

Asset: `Sources/WhoopCollector/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/Life.png`.
The same mark appears in the LifeMark image set. iOS applies its own icon mask.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iPhone app icon for a personal telemetry app named Life. Create one beautiful, distinctive, minimal symbol: a single flowing, softly sculpted ribbon that subtly suggests a lowercase l and an unfurling leaf, an elegant sign of vitality and continuity. Large centered symbol occupying about 64% of the square, recognisable at 32 pixels. Rich near-black evergreen background (#111C1A), pale luminous mint / fresh chartreuse symbol (#D3F5AE), extremely subtle sculptural shading and beautiful precise edges. Quiet, premium, contemporary, organic geometry, deliberate negative space. 1024x1024 square image, opaque and full bleed. No text or letters as typography, no wordmark, no heart, no ECG zigzag, no medical cross, no extra decorations, no grid, no mockup, no border. Do not round the image's outer corners: iOS applies its own mask. Single finished app icon, not a sheet of alternatives.
